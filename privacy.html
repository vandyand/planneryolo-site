Privacy policy

kalshi-backup rclone is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests the Google Drive drive.file scope, which only allows access to files the tool itself creates.
  • It uploads the owner's own backup files to the owner's own Google Drive. It does not read any other Drive content.
  • No data is collected from, shared with, or sold to anyone else. No analytics or tracking is used.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.
